data-store

A private rqlite (SQLite-over-HTTP) database, reachable over the internet behind a single bearer token. Point your own clients or agents at the SQL API, manage the data here, or try the demo.

Connection

—
Example: curl -H "Authorization: Bearer <token>" -d '[["SELECT 1"]]' <API base>/query
